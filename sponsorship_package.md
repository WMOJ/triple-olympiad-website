# WOSSHacks Sponsorship Package

## About the Event
In TBD 2027, White Oaks Secondary School will host WOSSHacks, a one-day hackathon organized by our Computer Science Club. The event brings together some of Halton's brightest students to collaborate, solve problems, and build innovative solutions. With over a hundred interested participants spanning grades 9 to 12, WOSSHacks showcases the strongest and most diverse STEM talent at the largest high school in Oakville.

- **100–200** expected participants
- **9–12** grades represented
- **1 day** of building, start to finish

## Why Us?
For our sponsors, it is a unique chance to showcase your brand directly to motivated, STEM-focused students, their families, and our alumni community. Allowing you to position your organization as a supporter of education, innovation, and the next generation of technical talent. Last year, over 200 students participated, and with your support, we aim to expand the event even further.
